01 · EVIDENCE

Source documents, bound immutably

Exercise records from your Carta export, the merchant-banker FMV certificate, and the official RBI reference rate for the exact exercise date — snapshotted once and locked to the calculation forever. No market-rate fallbacks, no silent substitutions.

02 · ENGINE

Deterministic to the paisa

Spread → perquisite under Section 17(2)(vi) → itemised TDS with surcharge, cess, interest and penalty. Pure decimal arithmetic against date-versioned rule packs that cite the statute — the same inputs produce the same rupee, every time.

The review screen puts evidence on the left and output on the right: certificate page, FX snapshot, and citations beside the full breakup and rendered forms. One explicit Approve & Export action — nothing leaves without it.

Everything the filing needs, in one export.

Each approved package ships as a ZIP with the PDFs, a machine-readable values sidecar, and a full audit manifest.

  • Challan ITNS-281 / 281N worksheet — values formatted exactly as the e-Pay Tax portal expects, with the 1961→2025 IT Act transition handled by rule-pack date branching.
  • Form 24Q + Annexure-II — quarterly TDS return data, fed by the CIN receipt you attach after deposit.
  • Form 12BA & Form 16 — the perquisite statement and the employee's certificate.
  • Form 3921 — generated automatically for ISO exercises on the US side.
  • FEMA & disclosure flags — OPI reporting, LRS limits, ~90-day repatriation and Schedule FA exposure, flagged and tracked against a compliance calendar.
  • Cap-table module — share classes, option pools, grants and vesting, so exercise events reconcile against the ledger they came from.
  • Append-only audit trail — who uploaded, confirmed, calculated, and approved what, and when; approvals freeze their inputs permanently.

Built like the liability depends on it. Because it does.

Getting withholding wrong costs interest, penalties, and audits. Regal's architecture treats every rupee as a legal claim.

The validation cascade

Before anything can be approved, an independent verifier — deliberately sharing no code with the calculation engine — recomputes every value printed on every form. A single mismatch locks the package and disables approval until it's resolved.

AI in exactly one place

AI reads one thing: the FMV certificate PDF, and its extraction is shown beside the source page for human confirmation. Everything that touches money or law is hard-coded, versioned, and deterministic. AI never computes a number.

You stay the filer of record

Regal generates; it never files. Every package requires an explicit human Approve & Export by your CA or admin, and the export tells you exactly what to pay, where, and by when.
